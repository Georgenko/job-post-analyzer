# Example Run
 
**Input:** public job posting - Senior Software Engineer for Machine Learning Platform
 
> DSK Bank is one of the leading banks in Bulgaria, with a great IT team of more than
> 250 IT talents, experiencing professional growth and development every day.
>
> Our Advanced Analytics and AI Department is building state-of-the-art technology
> (machine learning platform and models) in order to enable data-driven decision
> making for various advanced analytics uses cases / applications (e.g. pricing,
> churn, propensity-to-buy, campaign automation etc.). Machine Learning Platform is
> foundational solution that enables multiple use-cases inside the Bank: machine
> learning model development and deployment, customer offer computation and
> automating real-time customer journeys.
>
> We are looking for talented and self-driven professional, that will lead the team
> designing and developing our Machine Learning Platform (MLP). Our MLP is built on
> Azure Cloud, using Azure services, open-source tools and custom developed code. It
> is a great opportunity to really make an impact on banking!
>
> **Team Lead – Sr. Software Engineer for Machine Learning Platform**
>
> **Responsibilities:**
> - Owns the MLP architecture and roadmap
> - Align with Data Scientists, Data Engineers, Markets, IT Cloud & IT On Prem,
>   InfoSec & other teams on future development of Machine Learning Platform
> - Lead integration of MLP with other Bank's systems
> - Ensure stable operation of production environment, provide all required
>   observability stack, maintain runbooks and incident response procedures
> - R&D infrastructure & service improvements via new approaches, frameworks, etc.
> - Proposals for changes
> - Collaborate with the data scientists' team to improve the monitoring of the
>   machine learning models performance.
> - Participates in different digital projects and cross functional teams.
>
> **Expectations**
> - Strong python experience (sqlalchemy, fastapi, etc.)
> - Docker & Kubernetes knowledge
> - Understanding of software design patterns and testing
> - Ownership mentality, curiosity & pro-active approach
> - Verbal and written English
>
> **Ideal candidate**
> - Knowledge of relational databases (Postgres) and streams (Kafka)
> - Azure and Databricks experience
> - Experience with Java or Scala
>
> **Your challenges**
>
> We are building a Machine Learning Platform and we will require low-latency
> features- and data-stores for real-time model-serving. Hence there are a handful of
> challenges for our Development team:
> - Design and build micro-services to facilitate fast pace of change
> - Create state-of-the-art model serving architecture
> - Engrain development, testing & deployment best practices to reduce risks and
>   amount of mundane work
>
> **What we offer to you:**
> - Excellent opportunities for professional and career development in one of
>   Bulgaria's leading banks;
> - Food vouchers in the amount of 200 BGN per month;
> - 25 paid holiday leave;
> - Additional Health Insurance;
> - Favorable conditions for housing and mortgage lending, as well as for bank
>   products and services;
> - Annual bonus scheme depending on the achieved results;
> - Preferential conditions for Multisport / CoolFit card;
> - Great location;
> - Discounts in various companies;
> - Professional trainings for specific knowledge and skills (internal academies);
> - Refer a Friend Bonus.
>
> If you are challenged by this opportunity, we would be glad to review your
> application.
